Drag modules from the list into a semester to plan your Master's. Besides the Master's thesis and the _Masterpraktikum Bioinformatics_, you need at least 33 ECTS from _Methods and Research_ and 15 ECTS each from the two theory areas, for 120 ECTS in total. Areas that are still missing ECTS are highlighted in orange. Keep in mind that not every course is offered every semester.

Your plan is only stored in this browser. Use _Download_ to save it as a file and _Import_ to load it again.

:::note
The module list is unofficial and may be outdated. Here is the [official module catalog](https://www.cit.tum.de/cit/studium/studiengaenge/master-bioinformatik/modulkatalog/).
:::
